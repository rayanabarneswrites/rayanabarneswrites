About Me

Hi, I'm Ray.

Content strategist, word nerd, and firm believer that good copy shouldn't sound like a textbook.

The Short Version

I'm a content writer and SEO strategist with 7+ years of experience helping brands find their voice and their audience. I've worked agency-side, in-house, and remotely, managing everything from single blog posts to 100+ client portfolios.

My superpower? Making complicated things sound simple, boring things sound interesting, and invisible brands show up on page one.

The Longer Version

My journey into content started where many good things start: with curiosity and a graphic design degree from UNLV that taught me how to think visually, even when working with words.

I cut my teeth in the pre-ChatGPT era (yes, that's my flex), learning SEO fundamentals, content strategy, and the art of writing for humans and algorithms before AI made everyone a "content creator." That foundation matters because I actually understand why things work, not just how to make a tool spit something out.

Over the years, I've:

  • Launched new websites and watched them climb the rankings.
  • Managed content for 100+ brands across B2B and B2C sectors.
  • Collaborated with teams from SEO specialists to in-house counsel to subject matter experts.
  • Generated real revenue (we're talking $20K+ from content alone).
  • Stayed curious, and constantly learning, adapting, and staying ahead of industry shifts.
